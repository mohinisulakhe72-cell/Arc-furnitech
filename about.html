OUR STORY

Leading Office Furniture Manufacturer Across Vidarbha

Arc Furnitech is a market-leading office furniture manufacturer operating across the Vidarbha region, delivering premium ergonomic seating and workspace solutions for modern commercial environments.

Our fully-equipped 10,000 sq.ft manufacturing unit located in Pardi, Nagpur enables us to design and produce customized office furniture with precision craftsmanship and premium materials.

We believe modern workspaces should combine comfort, productivity and timeless aesthetics — helping businesses create inspiring environments.
